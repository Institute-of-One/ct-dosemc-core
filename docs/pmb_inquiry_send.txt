Subject: Pre-submission inquiry - Paper (not Note): TG-195-verified CT organ dose
versus organ-weighted CTDIvol on one multi-vendor cohort


Dear Editor,

I would like to confirm that the following manuscript is appropriate as a Paper,
rather than a Note, for Physics in Medicine and Biology.

Title. How far an organ-weighted CTDIvol index is from absorbed organ dose:
TG-195-verified Monte-Carlo measurement on the same patient anatomy across four
vendors.

Hypothesis. On the same patient anatomy, absorbed organ dose in mGy and the
organ-weighted CTDIvol index are different quantities. I measure that difference
after verifying the transport engine against AAPM TG-195 Case 4 with a stated,
replica-measured uncertainty.

Why this is a Paper. PMB defines Papers as original scientific research, covering
techniques and applications. The manuscript has both.

(i) Case 4 second-test agreement with PENELOPE, reported with a combined
uncertainty rather than a homemade pass or fail: at the centre position
|ours - PENELOPE| = 0.359%, which is 1.67 combined standard deviations. The
phantom-length term is measured, and 40 cm is indistinguishable from 160 cm. The
limitation budget is declared rather than assumed.

(ii) Absorbed organ doses on 32 TCIA series across four vendors, with a
pre-declared metal-implant exclusion, each scaled to a CTDIvol the series actually
carries: recorded for 23 of 32, reconstructed for 7, unrecoverable for 2. The
same-patient scatter is 12 organs and 342 points over 30 series, with Pearson
r = 0.66 overall and 0.61 on the 23 series carrying a recorded CTDIvol.
Restricting the figure to those 23 series does not change the relationship
between index and absorbed dose. The median absorbed dose is 24.3 mGy, ranging
from 3.9 to 84.0 mGy, and the median ratio of absorbed dose to index is 2.17.

That scatter is the application result, not a software demonstration. It is the
evidence that an organ-weighted index and an absorbed dose, computed on identical
anatomy, are correlated without being the same quantity.

Why this is not a Note. A Note would be the engine, or the TG-195 reproduction,
on its own. Those are Methods here. The claim is the measured difference between
two published endpoints on one cohort.

Related work, and why this is not salami. Two earlier papers from this line report
different endpoints on the same pipeline: whole-scan CTDIvol
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.21636719, v0.1.1) and an organ-weighted CTDIvol index
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.21817307, v0.1.1). This manuscript reports absorbed dose in
mGy, computed by Monte-Carlo transport rather than looked up. Three endpoints, one
pipeline; the scatter is the evidence that they are not slices of one number.

Length. Within the Paper limit of 10 000 words and 12 figures. Planned: five
figures and four tables.

If the Board would prefer a different article type, I would rather hear that now
than after review.
